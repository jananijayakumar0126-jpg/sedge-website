The five dimensions

Five questions,
asked of every contract.

D1
Risk / Reward
Payoff per dollar paid, if the view is right.
D2
Vol Cheapness
Cheap or dear against its own history.
D3
Skew Edge
Which side of the smile the trade sits on.
D4
Delta Fit
The exposure the view actually calls for.
D5
Catalyst Timing
Whether expiry catches the event.

Weighted into one score between 0 and 1. The best contract is rarely the cheapest or the highest-payoff — it's the one that answers all five well.
