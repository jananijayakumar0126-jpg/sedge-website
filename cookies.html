Cookie Policy

Last updated · 24 August 2026

How this website uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them.

01What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used to make sites work, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the site operator. Similar technologies — such as local storage — do a comparable job, and this policy covers those too.

02Categories we use

The cookies and similar technologies that may be used on this site fall into these categories:

  • Strictly necessary. Required for the site to load and function securely, and to remember your cookie choices. These cannot be switched off.
  • Preference. Remember choices you make, such as a dismissed notice, so the site behaves consistently between visits.
  • Analytics. Help us understand which pages are read and how the site performs, in aggregate, so we can improve it. These are only set with your consent.

We do not use advertising, retargeting or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not permit third parties to use cookies on this site to build advertising profiles of you.

03Third-party resources

This website loads fonts, stylesheets and JavaScript libraries from third-party content delivery networks, including Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr. These are used to render the site, not to track you. However, requesting a file from a third-party network necessarily discloses your IP address and basic request headers to that provider, which processes them under its own privacy policy. If you would rather avoid this, you can block those domains in your browser, though the site may not display correctly.

04Managing and disabling cookies

You can control cookies in several ways:

  • Most browsers let you see what cookies are stored, block them, or delete them — usually under Settings, Privacy or Security.
  • You can set your browser to warn you before a cookie is stored, or to refuse cookies entirely.
  • You can delete cookies already stored at any time.

Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of this website from working properly. Guidance for each major browser is available from its own support pages.

05Consent

Where required by law, non-essential cookies are set only after you consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing cookies in your browser and declining when next asked.

06Do Not Track

There is no agreed industry standard for how sites should respond to browser Do Not Track signals, and we do not currently respond to them. The controls described above remain available to you.

07Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as the site changes. The “last updated” date above shows the current version.
